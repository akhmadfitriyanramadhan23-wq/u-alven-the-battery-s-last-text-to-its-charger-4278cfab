Entrants write the phone battery's last text message to the charger it waited for all day, as one line of plain text of at most 200 characters.

> You charged your toothbrush, your watch, and your vape, then left me at 1% by the outlet all day. Goodbye. Tell the toaster I loved it more.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
